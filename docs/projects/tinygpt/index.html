How TinyGPT Works

TinyGPT breaks your prompt into characters, runs them through a stack of transformer layers, picks the next likely character, and repeats until it has a full answer!!!

The 12-layer stack looks back over the earlier characters and decides what kind of character should come next.

Pick A Character And Repeat

TinyGPT chooses one next character, adds it to the text, and runs the whole loop again until the response is complete.
