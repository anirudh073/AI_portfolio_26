# Monthly Summary — dec_2025

## Monthly Summary: December 2023

This summary covers research activities conducted throughout December 2023, focused on analyzing neuronal spiking activity in relation to movement and location.

**1. Speed Tuning Analysis (mPFC & OFC Neurons)**

*   **Progress:** Initial analysis of speed tuning curves for mPFC (33 cells) and OFC (133 cells) neurons from animal J16 was completed. Three distinct response patterns were observed: unresponsive cells, cells with increased firing rates, cells with decreased firing rates, and cells with bell-shaped responses.
*   **Methodology:** Firing rate curves were generated based on two run epochs. Confidence interval calculations initially assumed Poisson-distributed firing rates, but bootstrapping will be explored as an alternative.
*   **Future Directions:** The analysis pipeline is being extended to include waveform retrieval, spike raster plots, cell type identification (interneuron/pyramidal), and autocorrelogram analysis to assess speed modulation. Centroid computation using limbs, examination of animal location's influence on the observed effects (plotting journey speed distribution), and ordering cells by tuning are planned. The presence of inflection points in some tuning curves requires investigation.
*   **Code Development:** The `spike_analysis.py` script has been developed for single unit speed tuning analysis, including functionality to retrieve firing properties. (`spikes_analysis.ipynb`)

**2. Position Tuning & Movement Analysis**

*   **Analysis & Findings:** Analysis of mPFC neuronal firing rates revealed 15 significant units on the central arm during the outbound phase, 4 on all arms, 3 on the central arm during inbound phase and 7 on all arms. Tuning curves differed on the central arm between inbound and outbound phases.
*   **Methodology Enhancement:** The positioning analysis is being improved by decreasing bin size. Currently checking for firing rate at 40 cm linear position bins, which may be too large to observe tuning. Examination of firing rates in relation to "journey" progress is also planned.

**3. Data Acquisition & Processing**

*   **`.nwb` File Integration:** Encountered errors when inserting `.nwb` files due to mismatched probe descriptions. A script was developed using `h5py` to modify probe descriptions within the files, resolving the issue.
*   **Data Expansion:**  Efforts are underway to expand the dataset by incorporating data from animals Wilbur, Peanut, and Senor (prioritizing data amount). A YAML file has been created for Wilbur.

**4. Code and Workflow Improvements**

*   **Virtual Rat Code Review:**  A code review meeting is planned.
*   **Analysis Environment:** Explicit activation directions using `uv` are being implemented.
*   **Activation UI:** Potential improvements to the activation UI are being explored, including stepwise/typed inputs and additional plotting functions.

**5. Stability Testing Across Epochs (Wilbur - 20210512)**

*   **Methodology:** A protocol for assessing stability of tuning curves across epochs has been developed. This includes calculations of: curve correlation, normalised root-mean-square error (nRMSE), peak shift, mean firing rate difference.
*   **Initial Findings:**  201 out of 250 cells analyzed from Wilbur session 1 were deemed stationary, 58 showed drifting behavior, and 4 had insufficient data. `shape_drift` was observed in 10 cells, while `rate_drift` was present in 53. Results suggest stability of tuning curves.
*  **Null baseline**

    * median nrmse: 0.32066725526641193
