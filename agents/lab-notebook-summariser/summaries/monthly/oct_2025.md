# Monthly Summary — oct_2025

## Monthly Summary - October 2023 - Neuro-Magical Research Lab

This report summarizes research activities and findings from October 2023. Focus has been on characterizing neural circuits in multiple magical creatures, primarily Thunderwing Salamanders (TS), Frostback Hares (FH), Voidfish (VF), Gloomhounds (GH), and a newly introduced juvenile Emberwyrm (EW).

**I. Major Accomplishments & Milestones:**

* **Established baseline recordings:** Successfully established stable baseline recordings in multiple species, including TS-01, FH-02, VF-03, GH-04, and EW-05.
* **Rune-Shield implementation:** Successfully implemented and calibrated a rune-shield to reduce ambient aetheric noise, significantly improving recording quality.
* **Key Circuit Identification:** Identified key neural circuits involved in wing-flicker control in *TS*, thermal regulation in *FH* ("frost aura" mechanism), cloaking in *VF*, olfactory processing ("darkness index") in *GH*, and thermal regulation in *EW*.
* **Methods Section Completed:** Finalized the methods section for a forthcoming manuscript.
* **Colloquium Outline Completed:** Prepared an outline for a presentation at the Arcane Neuroscience Colloquium.



**II. Key Findings & Results:**

* **Thunderwing Salamander (TS):** Identified 18 dorsal spinal ganglion units with 3 exhibiting correlation to wing-flicker events.  Directional tuning to wing-flicker was found to sharpen significantly when the animal adopted a pre-flight posture.
* **Frostback Hare (FH):** Discovered a 4 Hz low-frequency oscillation in ear tissue, exhibiting a strong anti-correlation with ambient temperature and appearing to drive local cooling. This oscillation is likely related to the "frost aura" phenotype. Astral imaging confirmed mana-storage granules in ear tissue, synchronized with this oscillation.
* **Voidfish (VF):** Identified a dense cluster of "void-ganglion" cells that fire synchronized volleys during cloaking.  Null-rune injection successfully suppressed these volleys and abolished cloaking, confirming their necessity.
* **Gloomhound (GH):**  Observed a unique “inverted” tuning in the shadow-lobe olfactory system, with reduced firing to mundane scents but a paradoxical increase in response to phoenixash, supporting a "darkness index" encoding strategy.  This index appears linearly modulated by ambient light levels.
* **Emberwyrm (EW):** Identified distinct “heat-ON” and “heat-OFF” populations within the brainstem thermal nucleus, suggesting a system for active thermal regulation.



**III. Recurring Problems & Solutions:**

* **Ambient Aetheric Noise:** Initially high ambient aetheric noise during recordings was addressed by implementing a null-rune shield, reducing noise floor by ~40 dB.
* **Poor Cluster Quality:** Initial poor unit cluster quality in *TS* recordings was attributed to electrode placement errors. Subsequent repositioning of electrodes (using coordinates from histology) resulted in significantly improved data quality.
* **Astral Imager Calibration:**  Periodic drift in the astral imager required maintenance; a request was submitted and the imager subsequently returned.
* **Distress during Reagent Injection:** Observed distress in *VF* during null-rune injections; plan to use a lower dose for future replications.
* **Emberwyrm Heat Output:**  The unusually high heat output of *EW* required the installation of heat-dampening wards prior to recording.



**IV. Ongoing Threads & Outstanding To-Dos:**

* **VF Cloaking Replication:** Replicate the *VF* cloaking experiment with n=3, employing a lower null-rune dose.
* **GH Darkness Index Validation:** Further investigate the GH “darkness index” hypothesis with controlled light-level manipulation.
* **EW Thermal Circuit Characterization:** Continued characterization of the EW thermal regulation circuitry.
* **TS Motor/Sensory Differentiation:** Determine if correlated units observed in *TS* recordings are pre- or post-synaptic to wing motor plates.
